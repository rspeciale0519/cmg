About CMG

Licensed adjusters on the policyholder's side.

After a catastrophe, insurance carriers field thousands of claims at once — often with new, hurried adjusters. Things get missed, understated or denied. CMG Property Solutions exists to balance that table: experienced, licensed adjusters who read the fine print, document everything and advocate for the property owner alone.

We are licensed and bonded in every state where we work, bound by each state's code of ethics for public adjusters, and members of professional adjusting associations. Se habla español.

01

Integrity

Our fee agreement is in writing before we start, our estimates are documented line by line, and we never promise a number we can't support.

02

Communication

You will always know where your claim stands. We keep you informed at every step, answer your questions plainly, and return every call the same day.

03

Results

We are paid only from your settlement, so our incentive is yours: recover the full value of the loss and restore the property, as fast as the claim allows.

The team

Adjusters first, salespeople never.

Our team brings 45 years of combined experience across residential and commercial losses — carrier-side training, policyholder-side loyalty. Every file is worked by a licensed adjuster, not handed to a call center.
